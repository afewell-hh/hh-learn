Find out what Hedgehog does for an AI network

The network decides two things about an expensive GPU cluster: how much of its performance reaches the workload, and how quickly it becomes something teams and customers can actually use. Pick whichever question is closest to yours. Every route below opens on its own, in any order, with nothing to fill in.

  • Give every team and customer their own slice of the cluster

    An idle GPU-hour is the most expensive thing in the building, and what usually keeps one idle is not the hardware. It is how long it takes to get the next team, or the next paying customer, onto the cluster safely. Most networks are hard on the outside and soft in the middle, so each one is a project: shrink the blast radius and you have shrunk the wait as well.

    Cloud VPC networking made segmentation cheap enough to be the default. On hardware you own it is still hand work, because the tooling cannot keep up with how fast applications and facilities change.
