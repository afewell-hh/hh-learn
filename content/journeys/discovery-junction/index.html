Find out what Hedgehog does for an AI network

The network decides how much of a GPU cluster actually does work, and how long jobs take to finish. It is also the part that is hardest to get right. Pick whichever question is closest to yours. Every route below opens on its own, in any order, with nothing to fill in.

  • See the results from a GPU cloud that runs it

    SemiAnalysis test NCCL networking performance as one of their ClusterMAX criteria for GPU clouds. Their NCCL results from FarmGPU and RunPod, both running a Hedgehog and Celestica fabric, are the source of the published comparison in which those clouds came out ahead of NVIDIA's Spectrum-X reference cluster and an untuned RoCEv2 cluster.

    FarmGPU add their own account of the build: what the open fabric cost next to proprietary InfiniBand, and how long the cluster took to reach commercial launch. Their chief executive, JM Hands, chairs the Open Compute Project's neocloud working group. The numbers are on the pages that own them.

    Also: FarmGPU's own write-up · Hedgehog's analysis of the results · what the fabric is designed to do for AI workloads

    SemiAnalysis NCCL data, from their ClusterMAX testing of clouds that run this fabric. They rate clouds, not fabrics, so there is no SemiAnalysis rating of Hedgehog itself.

    Most networks are hard on the outside and soft in the middle. Cloud VPC networking made segmentation cheap enough to be the default. On hardware you own it is still hand work, because the tooling cannot keep up with how fast applications and facilities change.
